## Required native proof

A common geometric section is parameter-invariant: select one finite tangent direction T for the entire tied cluster and N=(-Ty,Tx). On each outgoing prefix, prove that `T?(C(t)-V)` increases strictly from zero, including stationary endpoints. Positive rational weights and nonnegative projected derivative Bernstein coefficients with at least one positive coefficient on each nonempty span are sufficient. An equivalent ordered Euclidean rational-control certificate must retain outward homogeneous cross-products and positive denominator bounds. Reversal changes the outgoing parameter orientation; affine or positive rational reparameterization must not change the conclusion.

Select one positive level reached by every prefix and isolate its unique scalar root on each original curve. Bound each N ordinate over its root enclosure, then refine until the ordinate intervals are disjoint. Increasing N at common positive T gives counterclockwise order. Pair-dependent levels in a sort callback do not supply a strict total order; the whole cluster needs one section.

This alone proves order **at that section**, not necessarily order immediately beside the vertex. Monotone curves A(x)=(x,x?) and B(x)=(x,2x?-4x?) have a common tangent, but B starts above A, crosses it at x=1/4, and is below it at x=1/2. Thus a section at x=1/2 reverses their germ order despite unique level roots. The prefix must have certified absence of another mutual intersection, or a direct ordinate-difference sign proof throughout the positive level interval. Being before the next retained event is sufficient only if discovery has already established that there are no omitted events; the current native curve-pair `complete` flag is not itself a formal root-count certificate. No result should be called certified by silently assuming that stronger prerequisite.

For the exact preserved quadratic fixture, the original numerator of `N?(C?-V)` has strict opposite signs throughout the open prefix, proved by Bernstein coefficient signs. That supplies a direct separating tangent ray and rules out another common point. Higher-order same-side branches need a stronger comparison or explicit unresolved handling; a raw parameter fraction or first unequal unnormalized derivative coefficient is not a replacement.

### Tangency regression that prompted the repair

The exact bowl `S=(x,y,x²+y²)` and line `C=(t,0.2,0.04)` have one isolated contact at `(0,0.2,0.04)`. The independent evidence exporter observed `complete=true` with 145 nearby contacts at tolerance `1e-6`. Axis-aligned hulls lose correlation between coordinates; tiny overlapping cells can therefore contain close points without containing a root. Leaf-clamped refinement could not move to the true contact.

The repair uses the original domains and solves the point equations together with the stationary-contact condition `normal(S)·C'=0` near singular candidates. It accepts the contact polish only at coordinate precision. It does not merge a broad tangent neighborhood. Added regressions cover the off-grid contact at two tolerances, a miss exceeding tolerance, two distinct nearby transverse roots, and shifted/tiny parameter domains. These additions are pending the root agent's serialized build at this audit checkpoint; they must not yet be reported as passed.

The first integrated run passed 129/133 groups, including all seven assembler groups, but both new CS groups returned `Unresolved curve/surface root enclosure`. Comparing the failed path with `math_FunctionSetRoot::Perform` at lines 817–827 and 1276–1417 exposed two remaining divergences: native Newton could accept a worsening clamped step, and strict ordinary-Newton acceptance prevented the separate stationary solver from seeing a near-tangent candidate. The next revision bounds steps to a quarter domain, backtracks worsening steps, retains the final candidate parameters even when the ordinary solve cannot certify a root, and lets the stationary solver independently certify that candidate. It still reports incomplete if neither solver validates. The shifted-domain regression passed on rerun; the combined off-grid group reached the unchanged subdivision work limit.

The work-limit failure exposed a separate candidate-stage divergence. `src/ModelingAlgorithms/TKGeomAlgo/Intf/Intf_InterferencePolygonPolyhedron.gxx:296–347` uses plane-aware triangle interference with deflection margins after AABB lookup; `src/ModelingAlgorithms/TKGeomAlgo/IntPolyh/IntPolyh_MaillageAffinage.cxx:1466`, `TriContact`, projects onto triangle normals and cross-edge axes starting at line 1578. The native AABB-only traversal retained many false cells around a tilted tangent plane. The next revision projects **all positive-rational control points** onto patch-normal and curve/surface cross-tangent axes. Disjoint outward-padded intervals certify disjoint convex hulls; overlapping intervals remain candidates. This uses exact rational hull containment rather than tessellated geometry, and does not increase the work cap. Exact inputs and resulting parameter/residual records now export to separate `build/native-geometry-tests/curve_surface_<fixture>.json` files so focused test processes cannot overwrite one another. This revision is awaiting a rerun.

## Separate certified ray/support module

`surface_ray.h/.cpp` is an independent native module; it does not change `brep_classify` or call OCCT. The support must have finite positive weights and be continuous across its internal knots. Original knot rectangles partition the entire natural domain. `nurbs_interval::surface_controls` supplies outward-rounded translated homogeneous Bernstein controls directly from the original surface; repeated floating-point trimming is absent. For dominant nonzero direction component k, the two equations are

```text
F_j(u,v) = D[k] * (N_j(u,v) - O[j]*W(u,v))
         - D[j] * (N_k(u,v) - O[k]*W(u,v)), j != k.
```

The equations are scaled together by the positive largest direction magnitude, with interval division retaining the represented input direction. Since W is positive, their zeros are exactly the line/support incidences. Their derivatives are polynomial Bernstein differences, avoiding independent numerator/denominator dependency in rational derivative bounds. A strict sign in either coordinate, or a strict common sign after any finite separating projection of all interval controls, excludes a cell. A certified negative dominant coordinate excludes support entirely behind the ray origin.

For a remaining rectangle, the implementation uses normalized parameters, an arbitrary finite numerical inverse R of the central interval Jacobian, and the interval image `-R*F(center) + (I-R*J)*Z`. A disjoint image excludes every root in the rectangle. Strict image inclusion in the **inner** normalized parameter bounds, together with an outward infinity-norm bound below one, proves a unique root by contraction. Original C0 knot crossings are allowed because the homogeneous equations are continuous and their one-sided derivatives are enclosed on every polynomial piece. Discontinuous supports are explicitly unsupported. An expanded chart may contain multiple original knot spans but is clamped to the original natural domain; consuming a candidate requires that the entire candidate be contained in that proven unique chart.

The image encloses the actual root and is contracted further for output accuracy. Newton polishing uses original surface evaluations and requires both small lateral residual and small full world displacement. It stays inside the unique chart. An independent outward world enclosure then bounds the reported point's distance to the actual root by the requested tolerance and bounds its ray distance. This final enclosure, not Newton alone, establishes association and output accuracy. Origin-straddling distance enclosures remain unresolved. Charts can be merged only if one certified root enclosure is contained in the other's proven unique chart; overlapping enclosures without such a proof produce incomplete status. Fixed UV or world-distance clustering is absent.
